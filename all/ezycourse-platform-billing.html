How money moves between EzyCourse, schools and payees

The first page followed a student paying a school. This page follows everything else: the school owner paying EzyCourse for a plan, a percentage of sales and storage; agencies charging their child schools; schools paying affiliates and sellers through Stripe Connect; and what EzyCourse staff do in the super admin dashboard. The running example is the same school, Pixel Academy (school_id 42), whose owner is Rafi (users.id 6000).
